Walk to End Alzheimer's Event Occurring in Door County

Read the full story here:

On several days throughout September, many people come together to help raise awareness about Alzheimer's, and to donate to the cause. It's a simple event with a few food trucks and raffle door prizes included. The walk is not very long, encouraging people of all ages to come and show their support.
The event will occur on the twelfth of September at Sturgeon Bay's Martin Park, and the nineteenth at Stella Maris Parish, in fish creek. Both events have an opening time of 8:30am, with the walk beginning at 10:00am.
An attendee of last year's Walk to End Alzheimer's said, "It's a nice way for people of all ages to come together and show support for everyone afflicted with this disease, and to help fund it’s research”

More information about this event can be found on the Sturgeon Bay City website.
