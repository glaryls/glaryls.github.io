        <h4>Read the full story here</h4></p>

        <hr />

<p>

    On several days throughout September, many people come together to help raise awareness about Alzheimer's, and to donate to the cause.

    <br />

The event will occur on the twelfth of September at Sturgeon Bay's Martin Park, and the nineteenth at Stella Maris Parish, in fish creek. Both events have an opening time of 8:30am, with the walk beginning at 10:00am.

<br/>

An attendee of last year's Walk to End Alzheimer's said, "It's a nice way for people of all ages to come together and show support for everyone afflicted with this disease, and to help fund it’s research”
